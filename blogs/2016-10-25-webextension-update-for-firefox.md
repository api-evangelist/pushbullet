Yesterday we released a very special update to the Pushbullet Firefox extension—our Firefox extension is now a WebExtension! WebExtensions are a cross-browser system for building browser extensions that is compatible with Firefox, Chrome, and Opera. (Soon Microsoft’s Edge browser will support them too.) Firefox added support for WebExtensions in Firefox 48 and this was terrific news for developers like us here at Pushbullet.
