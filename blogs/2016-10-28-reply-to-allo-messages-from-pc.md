Grab the latest Pushbullet for Android update to start replying to Allo messages from PC now! Allo (Google’s brand new messaging app) is missing one feature that those of us who spend most of their time on a computer really want—the ability to send messages from a computer. While it’s not a full desktop app for Allo, Pushbullet can now enable you to see and reply to Allo messages from your computer.
